# Portuguese website compliance — Tio Fredo

Reviewed 7 November 2026 against the official sources linked below. This is a scoped implementation audit, not a declaration that the business or website is fully compliant. Applicability depends on the operator, processing activities and services actually offered; there is no single checklist that makes every website legal.

The current React site presents the restaurant, menu, telephone/email contacts and an embedded Google map. Its search form filters dishes locally; no online checkout, customer account, reservation submission or newsletter subscription was found in the active routes. Phone/email contracting still needs its own assessment if used for distance sales.

## Conditional requirements

- **Online orders, paid bookings or other distance contracts:** assess pre-contract information, total costs, payment/delivery, cancellation, complaints and contract confirmation under [DL 24/2014](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/24-2014-572450). A generic terms page is not a universal requirement for all websites. Do not automatically promise 14-day cancellation for prepared/perishable food or date-specific restaurant services; exceptions can apply.
- **Accessibility:** covered services, including e-commerce, have duties effective from 28 June 2025. A presentation-only restaurant website is not automatically an e-commerce service; the law exempts qualifying service microenterprises. Determine scope and exemption before claiming compliance or publishing accessibility statements. [DL 82/2022, arts. 2.º and 39.º](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/82-2022-204379872).
- **Obsolete EU ODR/RLL links:** do not add the former EU dispute platform from old templates. It was discontinued, with repeal effective 20 July 2025. Portuguese RAL duties are separate. [Regulation (EU) 2024/3228](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/PT/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32024R3228).

## Information needed from the client

- Registered legal name, NIF/NIPC, registered address and relevant registry details.
- Electronic complaints-book registration and applicable RAL arrangements.
- Who handles enquiries; what data is retained, for how long and why; actual hosting/vendor arrangements; any analytics, advertising or mailing tools used outside this codebase.
- Whether phone/email contacts lead to distance orders, and whether online ordering/reservations are planned.
- Confirmation that published prices and allergen information remain current.

Policies and footer links are only the visible part: processing, consent enforcement, appropriate security, supplier agreements and business procedures must match the published information. New features, additional jurisdictions and regulated activities require a fresh assessment.
