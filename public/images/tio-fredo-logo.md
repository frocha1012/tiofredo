# Original Tio Fredo logo

`tio-fredo-logo.png` is the restaurant's original logo embedded on page 3 of the client-supplied `ementa-tio-fredo-julho-2026.pdf` (image object 1180, transparency mask 1814). The same artwork also appears on page 1.

The PNG preserves the original brown and gold pixels and transparency. Only the empty transparent margins were trimmed, leaving a two-pixel transparent border. Output size: 357 × 365 pixels. No background, lettering, or artwork was redrawn.
